✦ The Art of Formulation

The Architecture of Scent

Every Maison Aura fragrance is engineered in three harmonious olfactory movements, meticulously designed to unveil new dimensions as the hours progress on your warm skin.

Explore Notes Breakdown
01

Top Notes • The First Impression

0–15 Minutes. Volatile, sparkling citrus, luminous aldehydes, and invigorating rare spices that greet the senses upon initial misting.

02

Heart Notes • The Olfactory Soul

15 Minutes – 4 Hours. The emotional character of the perfume. Rich florals, hand-selected resins, and aromatics that define your presence.

03

Base Notes • The Enduring Resonance

4 – 12+ Hours. Heavy, precious molecules — aged oud, ambergris, Mysore sandalwood, and musk that fuse seamlessly with your skin's natural warmth.
